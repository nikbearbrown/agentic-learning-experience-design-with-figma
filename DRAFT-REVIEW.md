## Evidence Still Needed

RUN slots are intentional. They do not contain fabricated agent outputs, costs,
learner quotes, timings, or scores. Current Figma tools, plan/seat access, API bindings,
annotation read-back, and rate/credit behavior need primary-source and live checks.
The inherited references are research leads, not this book's completed claim audit.

The Figma snapshot/stamp helpers need actual live validation. The proposed rules in
Appendix D need real human correction reasons and round IDs before they can become
the approved Chapter 12 rules file. Proposed scripts, hooks, review skills, app builds,
and LMS mappings must not be presented as executed integrations.

Accessibility needs a current target standard/platform review and actual built tests.
The small learner study needs the appropriate institutional process, participants,
observations, and delayed responses. The capstone needs actual artifacts and its
own fair baseline before any measured-improvement claim.

## Consistency Points for the Next Editorial Pass

1. The existing Chapters 1 and 12 sometimes state broader tool/access generalizations than the new draft's dated/provisional wording. Harmonize after current research rather than silently treating older observations as current facts.
2. Chapter 1 permits continuing after helper failures; Chapter 2 requires missing critical evidence to be reported before a round is treated as complete. Decide the final incomplete-round/recovery policy.
3. Read Chapter 12's worked-solution release example alongside Chapter 5's distinction between a parallel demonstration and the target retrieval item.
4. Chapter 12's call allocation is approximate and unmeasured. Replace all chapter estimates with actual logs after live runs.
5. The strong Chapter 1 title and Chapter 6's "Can't Break" title are provocations. The prose does not claim guaranteed agent failure, guaranteed compliance, or measured learning from screenshots.
6. Local sibling-source pointers need to become self-contained chapter research notes and an author-reviewed bibliography before publication.

## Verification Boundary

The writing pass checks coverage, chapter structure, placeholder honesty, Markdown
rendering, and reading-copy assembly. It is not a substantive fact check or a human
rewrite. Every chapter sidecar remains `verified: false`. No production gate,
participant approval, publication decision, or human reason is signed by the agent.

The HTML and EPUB reading copies are compiled with Pandoc. Their provenance-method
link points to Appendix E so the reading copies do not depend on an unpackaged local
Markdown file. Source chapter links are unchanged. A visual browser preview could
not be performed because the available browser blocks local-file navigation; the
checks cover structure, links, parsing, and EPUB archive integrity, not visual layout.
